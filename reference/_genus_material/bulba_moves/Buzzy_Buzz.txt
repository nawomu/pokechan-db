Buzzy Buzz (Japanese: びりびりエレキ Shocking Electricity ) is a damage-dealing Electric-type move introduced during Generation VII , in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee! . It is one of the signature moves of the partner Eevee .

Trivia
Even though this move cannot be used in Pokémon Scarlet and Violet , one of the obtainable camera effects in the League Club Room is named after Buzzy Buzz. It shares this trait with Sizzly Slide .
