Baddy Bad (Japanese: わるわるゾーン Bad-Bad Zone ) is a damage-dealing Dark-type move introduced during Generation VII , in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee! . It is one of the signature moves of the partner Eevee .

Generation VII
Baddy Bad deals damage and sets up the effects of the move Reflect on the user's side of the field.
Generation VIII onwards
Baddy Bad cannot be selected in battle. However, it has been modified: its power was changed to 80 and its accuracy to 95%.
